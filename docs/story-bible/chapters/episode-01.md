**Source:** Uploaded `LUCID-Episode-1(1).pdf` (22 pages). This is a **visual/story synopsis**, not a replacement for the finished comic's lettering.

## Illustrated beats
- **Pages 1–9:** Lucid wakes remembering a nighttime city confrontation with a hooded, shadowy opponent. He fights using martial strikes, green ki/energy techniques and defenses; the hooded figure uses purple energy, a spectral beast and weapon. Lucid uses a powerful red attack labeled **Gusto Bang**. The figure departs as a large bird passes over the city.
- **Pages 10–12:** In the morning Lucid speaks to his big brother by phone. Big brother stresses responsibility, training, humility and that stronger opponents exist. He is shown in a cemetery setting.
- **Pages 13–16:** Lucid talks with his parents, who are driving. Their conversation covers his studies, a major project he has not completed, family expectations, and his brother's dangerous work. His sister is mentioned as investigating outside the city.
- **Pages 17–21:** His sister phones. They discuss crystals, caves, Ancient Towers, powerful monsters and Explorer history. Lucid continues preparing his invention.
- **Page 22:** Lucid notices a figure outside, approaches the window, then returns to work on his project.

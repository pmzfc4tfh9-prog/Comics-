# LUCID — Chapter 3: Fire Bear / The Ancient One

**Status:** Creator-established **plot beats**. Page divisions below are a working storyboard (pages 1–17), **not yet a final approved dialogue script**. Preserve the creator's intended action order and original character designs.

## Pages 1–6 — Vivid's Fire Bear
1. **STILL STANDING:** Immediately after Chapter 2's crater, Vivid is badly injured (head bleeding, torn clothes, bruised arms/torso) but still has fire. He stands. Ghost raises a hand and sends a **sideways fist from his right** that punches him into a wall.
2. **THE VIKING SHADOW:** A summoned Viking-style shadow warrior appears with **one huge long-handled axe**. Vivid thinks “What the—?” / slurs in shock, barely slips the axe strike, but suffers a **cut on his arm**.
3. **BREAKING POINT:** They meet near center. Vivid thinks **“When did she even raise her hand to summon him?”** He becomes enraged, activates the **Bear** spirit within his Link and transforms into a **Fire Bear**.
4. **BEAR'S RAGE:** Runs on **all fours** at the Viking shadow; **left** paw strike lands, then **right**, each producing fire on contact; **uppercut** connects, he **grabs the throat** and **slams** the shadow into ground. Illustrate each contact distinctly.
5. **FIRE BEAR ATTACK:** Bear jumps into air. **Three concentric fire rings** appear before its face, each progressively smaller. A **concentrated fire beam** passes through the rings and **cuts the Viking shadow in half**; it disappears. Attack name **not yet supplied**.
6. **HER LAUGHTER:** Ghost laughs. An enormous **overhead shadow fist** strikes Vivid and forcibly **knocks him out of Fire Bear form**. He returns to injured human form.

## Pages 7–9 — Void-fist combination
7. **THE VOID COMBINATION:** Vivid notices black hole close by and jumps aside, but a **fist shoots from it and connects**. Other portals appear, each with a fist that punches him from a different direction.
8. **RELENTLESS:** Successive portal punches land in a rapid **combo**. The penultimate punch launches Vivid **high into the air**; final punch from above drives him **downward**.
9. **SECOND CRATER:** Vivid smashes into the floor with a deafening impact and makes a **new, second crater** (distinct from Chapter 2 crater). Shock leaves him barely alive; body shakes.

## Pages 10–17 — Grace, the brand, Zephyra's rescue
10. **I STILL HAVE TO GET UP:** Vivid trembles on ground; eyes turn white from shock. He thinks that pain is everywhere and he can barely feel it, but **he must get up so he can go bowling**. He rises excitedly despite terrible injuries, driven by adrenaline.
11. **GRACE:** Vivid clenches fists, says **“Grace”**, ignites spiritual fire across his whole body in a magnificent **fire hemisphere**. He looks at Ghost, blinks, and she is gone.
12. **WHAT A WASTE:** Ghost has appeared **behind** him unnoticed. She touches his shoulder; fire **vanishes precisely where she grabs**. She says **“What a waste.”** Shoulder turns black with spreading mark. She throws him into center of room. Opens portal containing **a grasping hand, not a punching fist**.
13. **THE SHADOW BRAND:** Giant shadow hand **grabs and crushes** Vivid's body and spirit. Black mark spreads from shoulder over body; his fire is **drawn away toward Ghost**. He screams, remaining fire **fully disappears**, loses consciousness. The hand throws him into center.
14. **I AM NO GHOST:** Ghost takes hold of growing black hole. Addresses Vivid: **“I heard you, and I am no ghost.”** She says his techniques and people are of **ancient descent**, a rare sight nowadays. She looks into his eyes and memories.
15. **YOUR RIVAL:** Flashbacks of Vivid and Lucid. Ghost understands Vivid hunted a “ghost” to catch up to rival **Lucid**; says **Lucid isn't regular**, nor were Vivid's people before him; mocks his youth and hunting snakes and bears as **a waste**. She draws the enlarged black hole over unconscious Vivid, intending to swallow him.
16. **ZEPHYRA ARRIVES:** **Zephyra, Lucid's older sister**, arrives with two-ton staff and **red aura**. Eyes burn with anger and suppressed tears on seeing Vivid's injuries. She **strikes the black hole with her staff**, using strength and telekinesis, **redirecting it up toward space**. Do not invent an Angel transformation.
17. **THE ANCIENT ONE:** Zephyra recognizes Ghost as an **Ancient One** believed to have left the Tower years ago. Though furious, she avoids a direct fight. She throws a **blinding supernova-bright flash**, takes unconscious Vivid and leaves for a **hospital**. Ghost remains undefeated.

# LUCID — Chapter 2: “Give Me Two Hours” / Vivid vs. Ghost

**Sources:** Six illustrated opening pages marked **22–27**; creator's **16-page** continuation script labeled internal pages **7–22** (artwork production labels **28–43**). Creator's written script takes precedence over inaccurate generated artwork.

## Opening: illustrated pages 22–27
- **22:** Two days later, Lucid works on a device in his apartment. Board of Explorers messages him: considerable resources committed; some members doubt a kid; expects project ready by tomorrow. Lucid is frustrated.
- **23:** Lucid says he needs a break. Vivid calls from ancient ruins, in white-and-black martial gi with floating orange flame.
- **24:** Vivid confirms a ghost rumor is real; a long-haired female shadow materializes.
- **25:** Lucid explains Board pressure and says the invention is done except for tweaks; Vivid reacts to the delay while keeping eyes on the figure.
- **26:** Lucid wants to relax and go bowling. Vivid agrees reluctantly while facing the enemy.
- **27:** Vivid says **“Give me two hours. Give or take one.”** The call ends.

## Continuation — exact story order
### Internal page 7 / artwork page 28 — PATIENCE
Vivid pockets phone: **“Never knew ghosts had patience.”** Sets stance, small flame by shoulder: **“Sadly, I do have to wrap this up. I've got somewhere to be.”** Ghost screams in distorted static, shakes dust and rocks, raises hand, opens tiny black void.

### 8 / 29 — SOMETHING FOLLOWING
Pebbles and debris lift into void. It begins tracking Vivid. He **sidesteps and runs around a broken pillar** while it grows. He ignites forearms and charges Ghost. **SFX FWOOSH!** He does **not** smash his own face into the floor.

### 9 / 30 — FOUR FEET
At roughly **four feet** from Ghost, Vivid draws back a flaming fist. Ghost raises both hands, broad void opens, **colossal dark fist actually strikes Vivid**, with at least ten feet of arm extending. **SFX KRAAAM!**

### 10 / 31 — THROUGH THE STONE
Vivid crashes through **first** pillar (**KRAK!**), then **second** (**KRRASH!**), then embeds in **upper portion of far wall**, high above floor. Gi tears, blood bursts from mouth; from his blurred perspective giant fist retracts into void.

### 11 / 32 — SIX
Vivid is **still high in wall**, fading consciousness. Ghost screams and **six** shadow hounds materialize at her feet far below. Vivid reignites hand and thinks **“This can't be a ghost.” “This is something completely different.” “I hit the jackpot.”** His final expression is an **excited, wide grin**, not a grimace.

### 12 / 33 — UP THE WALL
Vivid: **“Stupid dogs. Let me get my senses together so I can catch up.”** Hounds charge **across floor and straight up vertical wall**. He sees jaws, tears free from upper wall and jumps away. Lands on floor; another hound slams him into **lower** wall (**THUD!**).

### 13 / 34 — CONTACT
Hound pins Vivid, snapping near face. Body ignites (**FWOOM!**), creature recoils, **Vivid grabs and holds it**, flames burn it into fragments. **Five remain.** Thinks **“Let me make quick work of these…” “…so I can get back to the main thing.”**

### 14 / 35 — THREE LEFT
Flame knives: Vivid stabs a lunging hound, dissolving it. Fire nunchucks: hits another (**WHAK!**). **Three remain.** Forms molten fireball overhead: **“Serpent's Fate.”**

### 15 / 36 — THE SERPENT
Fireball unfurls into giant burning python. Jaws snap (**KLAK!**), it bites first remaining hound. Rears on tail, spits fireball into second. **One hound remains.**

### 16 / 37 — BACK TO HER
Python returns to compact ball. Last hound leaps; Vivid slams ball onto it: **“Serpent's Fate!”** Last hound vanishes. Vivid turns; innumerable dark souls rise behind Ghost. Vivid: **“…What?”**

### 17 / 38 — FEEDING THE VOID
Ghost drives souls into roaming black hole; it grows to wrecking-ball size and pulls rubble. Vivid dashes at her. Both hands rise, giant fist returns. Vivid: **“Same trick can't work twice!”**

### 18 / 39 — VOLCANIC EMBER
Vivid backflips **over** the enormous fist, calls **“Volcanic Ember!”** Ghost slips clear and strikes him sideways through chamber wall (**KRAAASH!**). Black silent panel.

### 19 / 40 — NOT LIKE THIS
Vivid weakly floats toward black hole, flames gutter. Internal thoughts: **“This can't be it.” “Everything I fought for…” “Everything I learned. Everything I tested.” “My family. My friends. My future…”** Memory fragments show training and failure.

### 20 / 41 — TEN YEARS OLD
Flashback: both boys age **ten**. Vivid kicks, Lucid blocks/counters, launches him up, kicks him down. Vivid catches himself and fires two fireballs. Lucid dodges and replies with ki blast; clash holds evenly for a moment.

### 21 / 42 — I GOTTA CATCH UP
Lucid powers up and wins clash, then offers Vivid a hand. Present Vivid: **“I gotta catch up.”** Ignites **fifty** fireballs, sends barrage at Ghost, forms sphere matching black hole: **“Hawk's Grace!”**

### 22 / 43 — ALL I'VE GOT
Ghost draws void to herself, thrusts it against Vivid's huge sphere. She looks surprised. Vivid: **“I'll give you all I've got!”** He pushes ahead; Ghost chuckles and releases controlled aura, black hole pushes back. Vivid: **“I can't hold all of this…” “What is going on here?”** His arms fail, great sphere collapses, all flames extinguish. He falls into **first crater** (**KRRR—BOOM!**). **No final dialogue.**

## Production QA
Panel count, hound count, injuries, relative height, real contact and dialogue must match the script. Generated art drafts may not. Never add a “To Be Continued” speech bubble or replace the crater ending.

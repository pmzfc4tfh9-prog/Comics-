## PAGE 01 — STILL STANDING

**Panel 1 — WIDE:** Vivid lies in Chapter 2's crater, gi shredded, forehead bleeding, body bruised. A faint orange flame still burns.

**Panel 2 — CLOSE:** He pushes himself upright, trembling. The fire has not abandoned him.

**Panel 3 — IMPACT:** Ghost raises a hand. From Vivid's RIGHT, a massive shadow fist visibly connects and drives him into the wall. SFX: KRAAAM!

## PAGE 02 — THE VIKING SHADOW

**Panel 1 — REVEAL:** A towering Viking-like shadow materializes with a single enormous axe on a long staff.

**Panel 2 — REACTION:** Vivid, barely steady, thinks: “What the—?”

**Panel 3 — SLASH:** He narrowly slips the axe. Its blade still cuts his arm. Both fighters close toward the center of the ruins.

## PAGE 03 — BEAR LINK

**Panel 1 — THOUGHT:** “When did she even raise her hand to summon him?”

**Panel 2 — IGNITION:** Vivid's frustration turns to anger. Orange flame pours from his body.

**Panel 3 — TRANSFORM:** He activates his EXISTING Bear Link, transforming into a huge flaming bear. He drops onto all fours.

## PAGE 04 — THE CHARGE

**Panel 1 — WIDE:** Fire Bear launches across the chamber on all fours, claws ripping through loose stone.

**Panel 2 — LEFT HIT:** His LEFT paw strikes the Viking shadow. A burst of orange fire marks the actual contact.

**Panel 3 — RIGHT HIT:** Without stopping, his RIGHT paw connects. A second blast of fire erupts from the impact.

## PAGE 05 — THE CHOKE SLAM

**Panel 1 — UPPERCUT:** Fire Bear drives a flaming uppercut into the Viking shadow, lifting it off the floor.

**Panel 2 — GRAB:** Before the enemy escapes, Vivid seizes its throat in one huge paw.

**Panel 3 — SLAM:** He chokeslams the warrior into the stone floor. The ground cracks. SFX: BOOOOM!

## PAGE 06 — THREE RINGS

**Panel 1 — LEAP:** Still in bear form, Vivid jumps high above the wounded Viking shadow.

**Panel 2 — CHARGE:** Three fiery rings form in front of his open mouth: large, smaller, smallest, aligned in depth.

**Panel 3 — FINISH:** A concentrated fire beam fires through all three rings, cuts the shadow in HALF, and dissolves it. Technique name is not yet established.

## PAGE 07 — HER LAUGHTER

**Panel 1 — QUIET:** The Viking shadow vanishes. Ghost laughs rather than reacting with fear.

**Panel 2 — ABOVE:** A huge shadow fist appears DIRECTLY ABOVE the airborne Fire Bear.

**Panel 3 — BREAK:** It lands and forcibly cancels Vivid's Bear transformation. His battered human body falls. SFX: KRAAAM!

## PAGE 08 — THE FIRST PORTALS

**Panel 1 — DANGER:** Vivid notices the black hole alarmingly close and leaps away.

**Panel 2 — CONTACT:** A shadow fist shoots out and CATCHES him mid-dodge. Another portal opens and punches him again.

**Panel 3 — TRAP:** More holes appear around his body, lining up an inescapable combination.

## PAGE 09 — THE VOID COMBO

**Panel 1 — LEFT:** A fist connects from Vivid's left. His torso whips sideways.

**Panel 2 — BEHIND:** A second fist from behind catches him before he can recover.

**Panel 3 — ABOVE:** Another portal opens above his shoulder and strikes downward. Every punch must visibly connect.

## PAGE 10 — LAUNCHED

**Panel 1 — UPWARD:** A new portal fist drives Vivid HIGH into the cathedral chamber.

**Panel 2 — SUSPENSE:** He hangs above the ruined pillars for a moment, helpless.

**Panel 3 — DOWNWARD:** A final enormous fist emerges directly overhead and punches him toward the floor.

## PAGE 11 — THE SECOND CRATER

**Panel 1 — FALL:** Vivid falls past tall pillars; he cannot arrest his descent.

**Panel 2 — IMPACT:** He hits the stone floor. SFX: KRRRR—BOOOOM!

**Panel 3 — OVERHEAD:** The floor caves into a SECOND crater, separate from the Chapter 2 crater. His body shakes; his eyes whiten from shock.

## PAGE 12 — I STILL HAVE TO GET UP

**Panel 1 — CLOSE:** Vivid trembles in the crater, bruised and bloodied, barely able to move.

**Panel 2 — THOUGHT:** “So much pain everywhere… I can't even feel it.”

**Panel 3 — DEFIANCE:** “But I still have to get up so I can go bowling.” Adrenaline pulls him to his feet with an excited smile.

## PAGE 13 — GRACE

**Panel 1 — VOICE:** Vivid clenches his fists and says: “Grace.”

**Panel 2 — WIDE:** A hemisphere of blazing orange fire envelops his entire body, magnificent despite his injuries.

**Panel 3 — BLINK:** He looks at Ghost, blinks, and finds her gone from the spot where she stood.

## PAGE 14 — WHAT A WASTE

**Panel 1 — REVEAL:** Ghost is already BEHIND Vivid. She places her hand on his shoulder.

**Panel 2 — DRAIN:** All fire vanishes precisely at the contact point. She says: “What a waste.” The shoulder darkens.

**Panel 3 — THROW:** She hurls him to the chamber's center. A new void opens with a GRASPING hand—not a punch.

## PAGE 15 — THE SHADOW BRAND

**Panel 1 — CRUSH:** The giant shadow hand closes around Vivid and crushes both his body and spirit. He screams.

**Panel 2 — DRAIN:** The black mark spreads across his body as his remaining spiritual fire streams toward Ghost and disappears.

**Panel 3 — UNCONSCIOUS:** Vivid passes out. The hand throws his limp body into the middle of the ruins.

## PAGE 16 — I AM NO GHOST

**Panel 1 — APPROACH:** Ghost draws the grown black hole toward Vivid's unconscious body.

**Panel 2 — REVEAL:** Ghost: “I heard you. And I am no ghost.”

**Panel 3 — ANCESTRY:** She says his techniques and people are of ancient descent, rare in the present day, and looks into his memories.

## PAGE 17 — YOUR RIVAL

**Panel 1 — FLASHBACK:** Young Vivid and Lucid spar at age ten. Lucid wins, then offers Vivid a hand.

**Panel 2 — UNDERSTANDING:** Ghost understands Vivid hunted a ghost hoping to catch up with his rival Lucid.

**Panel 3 — THREAT:** She says Lucid is not ordinary, mocks Vivid's snakes and bears, and lowers the black hole to swallow him.

## PAGE 18 — ZEPHYRA ARRIVES

**Panel 1 — ENTRANCE:** Zephyra—Lucid's older sister—appears with her two-ton metal staff and a fierce RED aura.

**Panel 2 — EMOTION:** She sees Vivid's condition. Her eyes burn with anger; she fights back tears.

**Panel 3 — DEFLECTION:** Using strength, telekinesis and staff, she STRIKES the black hole and sends it upward toward space.

## PAGE 19 — THE ANCIENT ONE

**Panel 1 — RECOGNITION:** Zephyra recognizes the entity as an Ancient One, thought to have left the Tower years ago.

**Panel 2 — DECISION:** She chooses Vivid's life over fighting. A blinding supernova-bright flash fills the ruins.

**Panel 3 — ESCAPE:** She retrieves unconscious Vivid and escapes. Ghost remains undefeated. No Guardian Angel or Trinity Fusion is activated.

## PAGE 20 — THE ESCAPE

**Panel 1 — NIGHT:** Zephyra rides a SIX-FOOT-TALL tiger through a dark forest. Vivid lies in front of her, critically injured.

**Panel 2 — SHOUT:** Zephyra: “Vivid, hold on! We're going to get you back to the city!”

**Panel 3 — DETAIL:** The tiger charges between trees. Vivid's torn gi, swollen hand, head wound and shadow mark remain visible.

## PAGE 21 — A FEW MONTHS EARLIER

**Panel 1 — FLASHBACK:** A few months earlier, Zephyra and Vivid sit at a table having lunch.

**Panel 2 — VIVID:** “What's up, Zef? What's this all about?”

**Panel 3 — ZEPHYRA:** “I'm pressed for time, Vivid. I have to make this quick, okay?” She acknowledges his rivalry and his kind heart.

## PAGE 22 — THE RUMOR

**Panel 1 — FAMILY:** Zephyra explains that her family runs the paper and knows something about Vivid's clan abilities.

**Panel 2 — RUINS:** She describes a cave leading to ruins, rumored to be haunted by a vengeful spirit or ghost.

**Panel 3 — SHOCK:** Vivid: “Say what? That's crazy—a ghost?” Zephyra says ghosts are real; she has encountered them.

## PAGE 23 — TWO SLOTS

**Panel 1 — QUESTION:** Zephyra asks how many spaces remain in Vivid's animal Link. He has TWO unfilled slots.

**Panel 2 — UNCERTAINTY:** Vivid: “A ghost? I only ever tried animals.” She suggests there is more to discover.

**Panel 3 — SECRETS:** Zephyra: “There's a lot you don't know, Vivid. About you. About your own family.” She produces an item to examine together; its identity is not confirmed.

## PAGE 24 — THE CITY AHEAD

**Panel 1 — RETURN:** Back to the present: the tiger emerges from the dark forest into an open field.

**Panel 2 — DISTANCE:** The distant city rises beyond the grassland as dawn breaks. Zephyra supports Vivid.

**Panel 3 — PROMISE:** Zephyra: “We're getting closer.” The story continues with Chapter 4's city-gate emergency.

- The Viking shadow has one enormous long-handled axe; it is distinct from Chapter 2's six hounds.

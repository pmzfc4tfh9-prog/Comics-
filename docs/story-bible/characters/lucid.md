# LUCID — Main protagonist

**Design lock:** Teenage boy, warm brown complexion, short dense curly hair, expressive brown eyes, athletic build. Established casual appearance: black T-shirt and dark trousers. Use the original Episode 1 art and Chapter 2 opening pages; do not substitute a different anime character.

**Family:** Zephyra is his **older sister**. His parents and big brother appear or are mentioned in Episode 1; their personal names are not established in this record.

**Friend/rival:** Vivid. The two trained together when they were **ten**. Lucid blocks Vivid's kick, counters with a punch and aerial kick, dodges his fireballs, then wins their ki-vs-fire energy clash after increasing his output. Lucid offers Vivid his hand afterward.

**Powers:** Original combination of **ki and chakra**. Ki powers body, aura, energy blasts and limit-breaking; chakra powers techniques and elemental expression. Episode 1 shows green energy attacks/shielding and a red attack lettered “Gusto Bang” (verify final lettering). Existing `docs/power-system.md` controls unlock/mastery rules: sage pact, Super Sage, starting affinities and god-tier status must not be invented.

**Story:** Episode 1 opens after a night battle with a hooded stranger; family discussions establish the Ancient Towers and Explorer responsibilities. Chapter 2 opening shows him working on an invention under Board of Explorers pressure for a next-day reveal. He speaks to Vivid by phone and asks for “two hours,” give or take one. In Chapter 2's climax he appears in Vivid's childhood flashback, **not** at the ruins in the present. No present-day arrival at the Chapter 3 fight has been established.

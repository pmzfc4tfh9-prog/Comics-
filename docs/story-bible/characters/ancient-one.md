# ANCIENT ONE — the figure Vivid calls “Ghost”

**Identity:** Feminine shadow entity; called “Ghost” during the fight, but says **“I am no ghost.”** Zephyra recognizes her as an **Ancient One**, believed to have left the Tower years earlier. Individual name, motive, power limits and relation to the Episode 1 hooded fighter **not established**.

**Design lock:** Tall, feminine near-black silhouette; long ragged, flowing shadow hair; tattered dark outline shedding fragments/glitching static; unsettling eyes. Ancient ruined chamber, broken pillars, high walls and shafts of daylight. Never replace her with a hooded male or a normal human woman.

**Demonstrated powers:** Static-like scream shaking ruins; tiny roaming black hole that follows Vivid, absorbs debris and grows; souls summoned and fed into hole; massive shadow fists projected from portals, arm extending at least ten feet; multi-directional portal-fist combinations; six shadow hounds that run vertically up walls; Viking-like shadow warrior with single huge long-handled axe; rapid movement and physical blows; controlled aura overpowering Hawk's Grace; ability to touch Vivid's shoulder to extinguish flame locally, inflict spreading black shadow brand, and pull away his spiritual fire; giant grasping hand that crushes body and spirit; reads/evokes memories of Vivid and Lucid training.

**Chapter 3 reveal:** After Vivid is unconscious she explains that his techniques and people are of **ancient descent**, recognizes his rivalry with Lucid, says Lucid is not ordinary, calls Vivid's snake/bear hunting a waste, then tries to swallow him with the enlarged void. Zephyra interrupts and escapes with Vivid. Ancient One is **not defeated**.

**Summons:** Six hounds (destroyed by Vivid in Chapter 2), a separate Viking axe shadow (destroyed by Fire Bear in Chapter 3). Do not merge the two types or imply the Viking is one of the six hounds.

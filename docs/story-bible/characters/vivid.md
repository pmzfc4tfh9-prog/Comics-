# VIVID — Martial Flame

**Design lock:** Teenage martial artist with **buzz cut**, tan/brown skin, amber-brown eyes, athletic build. White kung-fu gi with black lapels/trim and belt, black hand wraps, dark loose trousers and boots. Small floating **orange flame** by shoulder. Clothes become torn and bloodied progressively; wounds persist. Use supplied Chapter 2 pages, not generic substitutions.

**Personality:** Skilled, confident, competitive and sarcastic. Excited by strong opponents: when he thinks **“I hit the jackpot”**, draw an exuberant grin and bright eyes despite injury. His determination to go bowling is a recurring ironic beat.

**Link/animal powers:** Internal flame combined with kung-fu; excellent eyesight. Five animal essence slots: **hawk, bear, snake**, plus **two still unassigned**. Can channel animal abilities without fully transforming; full transformation is also possible. Do not fill unnamed slots.

**Techniques:** Flame knives, fire nunchucks, fireballs and full-body ignition. **Serpent's Fate**: fireball turns into blazing python, bites/spits fire and reforms as compact ball. **Volcanic Ember**: named aerial fire attack that Ghost dodges. **Hawk's Grace**: 50-fireball barrage followed by huge fire sphere matching black hole. **Fire Bear**: massive flaming bear charges on all fours, lands alternating burning paw blows, uppercut, throat grab/slam, then jumps and fires a mouth beam through three concentric shrinking fire rings. Beam name **not established**. **“Grace”** in Chapter 3: full-body fire hemisphere; do not conflate with Hawk's Grace without approval.

**Relationship:** Lucid's childhood friend and rival; trains to catch up. Zephyra is **Lucid's sister**, who rescues Vivid.

**Critical choreography:** Chapter 2: colossal void fist **hits** Vivid from ~4 feet away; sends him through **two pillars** and embeds him **high in the upper wall**. Six hounds form below and **run vertically up the wall**. He leaps away, lands, is hit into lower wall; **grabs the first hound and burns it while holding it**. Five remain, then knives/nunchucks kill two and Serpent's Fate kills three. He loses Hawk's Grace collision and makes first crater. Chapter 3: side fist, Viking axe slash, Fire Bear victory, overhead fist cancels Bear, multi-portal punch combo creates **second crater**, “Grace” ignites, Ghost suppresses and brands shoulder, crushing shadow hand extinguishes his fire and he loses consciousness.

**Injury continuity:** Head bleeding, arm cut, extensive bruising, torn gi, repeated wall/crater impacts. No unexplained instant recovery.
